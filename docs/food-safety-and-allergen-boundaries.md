# Food safety and allergen boundaries
Ingredient declarations and user-entered dietary tags are informational. Unknown declarations remain unknown. Cross-contamination and medical safety cannot be inferred. Public safety claims need authoritative source IDs and review metadata. Personal instructions do not certify safe preparation or storage.
FDA storage guidance was checked on 2026-10-05 (engineering source verification, not clinical review): https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/are-you-storing-food-safely . It supports prompt refrigeration, two hours at room temperature or one hour above 90 F, refrigerator at/below 4 C/40 F and freezer -18 C/0 F. These are general guidance, not a determination that an individual leftover is safe. The source uses 90 F, approximately 32.2 C; the specification's rounded 32 C is not treated as an exact conversion.
