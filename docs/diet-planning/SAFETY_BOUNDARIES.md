# Supported-use boundaries

Ages 19–100, nonpregnant and nonlactating healthy adults, with explicitly entered age, equation sex, height, weight and PAL category. Professional-review limitations are displayed before calculation and require acknowledgement. No diagnosis history is collected.

The supplied low-intake and BMI boundaries, goal-specific adjustment ranges, larger-adjustment warnings and macro feasibility checks apply before save. Manual overrides require a reason and remain in the standard adjustment envelope. The minimum is a software boundary, not a nutritional adequacy assurance. AMDR differences are labelled rather than forced into range. Even meal distribution is convenience, not a biological rule.

No therapeutic diet, supplement prescription, micronutrient diagnosis, water-per-bodyweight prescription, body-fat-from-BMI value, guaranteed weight-change rate, exercise-calorie add-back or goal-date forecast is produced. Unavailable Phase 08 references remain unavailable.
