# Sources and rights

Framework definitions were checked against [NIH ODS recommendations](https://ods.od.nih.gov/healthinformation/nutrientrecommendations.aspx), the [FDA label reference explanation](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/daily-value-nutrition-and-supplement-facts-labels), [EFSA DRV definitions](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/topic/dietary-reference-values), [National Academies CDRR context](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/25353/chapter/15), and [ICMR-NIN publication descriptions](https://nin.res.in/popular.html). RAE, DFE and NE context was checked in the linked NIH ODS professional fact sheets. Glossary records contain immutable IDs/slugs, links and an explicit Codex source-check record dated 2026-10-05. This source check is not expert scientific signoff on intake datasets.

The registry retains separate primary authorities and their usage/rights policies. Every article citation has a specific locator and actual accessed date; public numeric rows additionally require a version-bearing citation and approved dataset/rights manifest. Composition is read from Phase 07, whose pinned USDA releases and source records remain authoritative.

No NIH, EFSA, National Academies or NIN page was copied wholesale. No paid NIN tables were scraped or redistributed. The Indian framework remains unpopulated and pending structured-use rights review. No numeric intake values, medical symptoms, interactions, training claims or reviewer identities were invented for public articles. Engineering fixtures are explicitly synthetic and remain under tests only.
