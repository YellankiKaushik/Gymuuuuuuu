# Supplements evidence and local records

Phase 14 has twenty routes. All 272 supplied draft identities remain outside the public index. No approved ingredient evidence, dose, AIS group or ingredient-specific anti-doping clearance is fabricated. Publication requires exact claim scope, cited sources, review identity/date and rights review. Build validates the production index.

The twelve-store browser database preserves label versions, embedded bounded images, intake snapshots and correction history. Products and trials use revisions to reject stale saves. Urgent suspected events atomically stop linked trials. Intake totals preserve form/unit/disclosure and are explicitly incomplete, with no medical clearance or automatic nutrition credit.

Settings exports JSON, seven CSV datasets and raw recovery. Imports are parsed and graph-validated before a confirmed atomic write. Keep-existing conflicts are recorded; replacement can repair malformed records. All stores participate in one transaction. Global backup and analytics use `supplementsBackupAdapter` and `supplementsReadModels`, respectively; those modules must preserve external owner IDs and never infer causality.

General safety resources are linked to official FDA and NIH pages. Reporting is manually offered for the United States; unreviewed jurisdiction reporting links stay unavailable. No automatic external requests, brand recommendations, unsafe compound protocols, diagnosis, payments or authentication are introduced.
