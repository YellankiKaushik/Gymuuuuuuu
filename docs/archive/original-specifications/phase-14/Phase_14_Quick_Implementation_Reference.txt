CORE RULES
- Claim-level evidence, never one global verdict.
- No invented claims, doses, interactions or anti-doping status.
- Study protocol is not personal prescription.
- Efficacy, safety, product quality and anti-doping remain separate.
- Certification must be checked in the official registry.
- Certification reduces defined risks; it never proves efficacy or zero risk.
- WADA status includes list year and expires when the year changes.
- Proprietary-blend amounts remain unknown.
- Product label edits create immutable versions.
- Intake logs snapshot the exact label version.
- Severe adverse-event signals stop normal guidance.
- No brand recommendations, shopping links, stacks or drug-like protocols.
- Personal records remain local.

SEED STATUS
All seed ingredient, knowledge, high-risk and framework identities are draft. They are not automatically publishable scientific content.
