104   EXERCISE & PHYSICAL ACTIVITY 
Weekly Exercise and Physical Activity Plan 
Use this form to make your own exercise and physical activity plan — one you think you really can manage.  
Update your plan as you progress. Aim for moderate-intensity endurance activities on most or all days of the  
week. Try to do strength exercises for all of your major muscle groups on 2 or more days a week, but don’t  
exercise the same muscle group 2 days in a row. For example, do upper-body strength exercises on Monday,  
Wednesday, and Friday and lower-body strength exercises on Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday. Or, you can  
do strength exercises of all of your muscle groups every other day. Don’t forget to include balance and  
fexibility exercises.
